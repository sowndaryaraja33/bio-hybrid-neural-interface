Bio-Hybrid Neural Interface

A browser prototype of a Brainoware-style pipeline: a spoken vowel is encoded, "delivered" to a simulated neural organoid through electrodes, and a machine-learning readout reports what it heard. Every organoid and neuron shown is a computational stand-in, not living tissue.
